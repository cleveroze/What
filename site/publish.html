Publish

Send your extension for review. When @cleveroze approves it, it appears in the Workshop for everyone — usually within a minute.

1 · Choose your extension

Faster from the app: in Stride open Extensions, press ⋯ next to your extension and choose Publish to the Workshop.

2 · Check

    3 · Send for review

    Opens a review request on GitHub under your account (a free GitHub account is needed). You will get a notification when it is approved.

    1Automatic checksFormat, permissions, size — within a minute.
    2Review by @cleverozeCode and permissions are read by a person.
